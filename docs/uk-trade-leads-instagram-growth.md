## Who should want to follow

UK Trade Leads helps owner-run UK trade and home-service businesses get found under their own name, earn trust from their work and reviews, and give direct enquiries a clear next step. Business-in-a-Box connects the website, Google presence, reviews, business phone, quote forms, inbox and follow-up. Done For You adds ongoing marketing and administrative support. The first useful sales outcome is a conversation about the owner's trade, area and current problem. Establish current offer details from the live site before using them in content. [Current offer](https://uktradeleads.com/) · [How it works](https://uktradeleads.com/how-it-works/)

Start with owner-run roofing and exterior businesses, and plumbing and heating firms. Both give us specific situations owners recognise from the tools, and UK Trade Leads has relevant published guides and examples. Keep the wider audience open to UK home-service firms with the same problems. A homeowner shopping for a roof, a marketing vendor and an ambiguous/private account are different audience evidence.

**Proposed reason to follow:** Practical fixes for UK trades to get found, handle enquiries and earn repeat work. Each piece should show something the owner can use that day, such as a reply they can copy, a check they can do on their phone, or a sum they can work out with their own figures. Jack on camera and a real example make the account recognisable. Use cleared client work, public demonstrations and clearly labelled examples. The profile name, bio and pins are proposals for review.

### First new Reel for review

**Working title:** You sent the quote. When do you follow up?

**Jack, in a fresh vertical take:** “Sent a quote and heard nothing back? Give yourself a date to check in. Put the date you sent it and the type of job into our free quote follow-up planner. It gives you three suggested dates and messages you can adapt. If you promised to call on Friday, stick to Friday. If they reply, take it from there. Follow for more practical fixes for your trade business.”

**Film it:** One natural 25–35 second take, with a second take for choice. Open on Jack with room for the complete title and a natural face frame for the Reel cover.
