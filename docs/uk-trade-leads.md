# InstaAddict for UK Trade Leads

This deployment belongs to UK Trade Leads. It operates
[@uk.tradeleads](https://www.instagram.com/uk.tradeleads/) to reach relevant UK trade
and home-service businesses, build useful awareness and support qualified enquiries
for UK Trade Leads. It is an acquisition channel within that business.

## Project and operating sources

The separate T3 Code project is **InstaAddict · UK Trade Leads**, rooted at
`~/GitHub/instaaddict` on Jack's MacBook. The old Documents checkout path links to
this same directory. The project provides manual status, pause and resume controls.
The UKTL website's T3 project also has an **Instagram growth status** shortcut.

- [Daily operation](daily-growth-macos.md) describes the schedule, configured caps,
  prepared comments, reservations and fault handling.
- Private `accounts/uk.tradeleads/growth/operation-status.json` holds the current
  owner, result, supplies and next checkpoint. Read it before live work.
- Private day journals, saved sessions and exact UI/comment receipts prove actions.
  An old completed run does not prove today's run completed.
- The daily maintenance task belongs to this T3 project. It verifies actual results
  and owns prospect replenishment and preparation of contextual comments.

Use the existing logged-in account and the reviewed UK business queue. Keep DMs,
publishing and unfollows disabled. Do not start another worker or replay a started
day when entering a new project conversation. Read the current private settings
rather than treating upstream examples as this account's permission.

This registration connects the project, its operating context and its daily

contract; page views and CTA clicks are not accepted leads or booked appointments.

## What success means

Check two kinds of evidence separately:

1. **Reliable operation:** the intended day's verified actions, account restrictions
   or faults, source-checked queues and next owner/checkpoint.
2. **Business movement:** relevant visits, genuine enquiries, useful conversations,
   attended calls and paying clients where supported by their actual sources.

Do not claim follower growth, new leads or sales from likes and follows. Keep
attribution unknown when it has not been verified. Use observed relevance and
commercial results to judge the channel rather than increasing volume by itself.
